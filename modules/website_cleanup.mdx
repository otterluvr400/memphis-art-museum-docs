## Business Logic

### Payment status skip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transaction monitored">
    `/payment/status` checks `_get_monitored_transaction()` from `payment` module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Already final?">
    If transaction state is `done` or `authorized` and not yet post-processed, the controller calls `_post_process()` with `website_cleanup_defer_invoice_send=True`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-process or fallback">
    On success, redirects to `monitored_tx.landing_route`. On `psycopg2.OperationalError` / `IntegrityError` or any other exception, rolls back and falls back to the native status page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice delivery deferred">
    The `_send_invoice()` override skips the immediate email send. The existing `_cron_send_invoice` cron delivers the invoice later because `is_move_sent` stays `False`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Payment processing spinner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Token flow initiated">
    `PaymentForm._processTokenFlow()` is patched to inject a blocking overlay div into `document.body`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Overlay persists">
    The overlay (`#website_cleanup_payment_overlay`) shows a Bootstrap spinner and "Processing…" text with a dark backdrop. It remains until browser navigation completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Native Odoo 19 Comparison

### `payment.transaction._send_invoice`

**Native Odoo 19:** Sends the invoice email immediately when the transaction is post-processed.

**This module:** Skips the send when `website_cleanup_defer_invoice_send` context key is set, relying on `_cron_send_invoice` to deliver later.

**Why it matters:** Prevents the request from blocking on email construction during payment finalization. The invoice arrives asynchronously.

### `/payment/status` page

**Native Odoo 19:** Always renders the payment status page, which auto-refreshes until the transaction is final.

**This module:** For already-final transactions, post-processes immediately and redirects to the landing route, skipping the status page entirely.

**Why it matters:** Reduces perceived latency for the customer after payment completion.

### `PaymentForm._processTokenFlow`

**Native Odoo 19:** Initiates the token flow without visual feedback beyond the payment provider's own UI.

**This module:** Adds a blocking overlay so the user cannot interact with the page while the payment is being processed.

**Why it matters:** Prevents double-submits and confusion during payment processing.
