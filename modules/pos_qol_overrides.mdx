# Business Logic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Session start">
    `pos_store.js` patches `PosStore.openOpeningControl()` to auto-confirm the opening control popup with zero cash and empty notes, bypassing the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cart interaction">
    `order_summary.js` makes Backspace in quantity mode remove the selected orderline instead of decrementing quantity. `product_screen.js` removes `-`, `discount`, `price`, and decimal-point buttons from the numpad and adds a `payCash()` helper that sets `pos.payWithCash` and navigates to payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment screen">
    `payment_screen.js` consumes the `payWithCash` flag in `onMounted()` to pre-select the cash payment method and remove an empty auto-created payment line when present.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Event registration popup">
    `event_registration_popup.js` adds:
    - A `Specify Tickets` toggle that hides tickets 2..N by default and fills empty answers from ticket #1.
    - An `Override - Anonymous` panel that records a `pos.anonymous.override` with category and reason, then blanks and fills mandatory answers with defaults (`tickets@memphisartmuseum.org`, `9015446200`, or `-`).
    - A blocked-domain guard (`@memphisartmuseum.org`) that rejects confirm unless an anonymous override is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order paid">
    `pos_order.py`:
    - `action_pos_order_paid()` sends an immediate `email_template_pos_event_order_confirmation` mail for event-ticket orders with a paid total and a registration email.
    - `send_paid_order_mail()` (called by `pos_event`'s `action_pos_order_paid()`) overrides the core per-registration ticket flow by creating batched `pos.ticket.email.batch` records (chunk size 25) and triggering the cron.
    - `event_registration.py` suppresses normal event mail schedulers for registrations that have a `pos_order_id`, so the order-level and batch-level emails do not duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ticket email batching">
    `pos_ticket_email_batch.py`:
    - `queue_order()` creates at most one set of batch records per `pos.order`, splitting registrations into 25-ticket chunks.
    - `_cron_process_pending_batches()` runs every 5 minutes, renders `event.action_report_event_registration_full_page_ticket` per registration, attaches PDFs, sends one mail per batch, and unlinks the attachments after send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Guest check-in / presence">
    - `pos_order.py` exposes `action_mark_registrations_present()` (toggle all non-cancelled registrations between `draft` and `done`) and `lookup_registrations_by_reference()` (find an order by `pos_reference`).
    - `event_lookup_popup.js` uses these RPC methods for a `Guest Check-In` dialog.
    - `receipt_screen.js` adds a `Mark as Present` / `Here` button and auto-marks present when the event/slot start is within ±12 hours of now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer display">
    `customer_display_adapter.js` and `customer_display.xml` mirror the member-pricelist discount line from the orderline patch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# Troubleshooting

- **Immediate confirmation email not sent** — Verify the mail template `pos_qol_overrides.email_template_pos_event_order_confirmation` exists and the order has a paid total and at least one non-cancelled event registration with an email address.
- **Ticket batch emails stuck** — Ensure cron `pos_qol_overrides.ir_cron_pos_ticket_email_batches` is active and the mail server is configured. Check `pos.ticket.email.batch` records in `error` state for SMTP/report failures.
- **No PDF attachments** — The batch renderer relies on `event.action_report_event_registration_full_page_ticket`. If the `event` module or report is missing, batches will fail.
- **Cash button not pre-selecting** — `pos.payWithCash` is a one-shot flag set by `payCash()`. It must be consumed before `PaymentScreen.onMounted()` runs; otherwise payment methods are selected normally.
- **Swipe-to-delete not working** — Only enabled on small screens (`ui.isSmall`), `display` mode, and when line quantity changes are allowed (`!pos.disallowLineQuantityChange()`).
- **Blocked domain error** — Confirming an event registration with an email ending in `@memphisartmuseum.org` requires an active anonymous override signed by a cashier.
- **Guest lookup menu missing** — The `Guest Check-In` dropdown item is added to the POS navbar by `navbar_guest_checkin.xml`; verify `pos_qol_overrides` is installed and assets are rebuilt.

# Native Odoo 19 Comparison

### `EventRegistration._update_mail_schedulers`

**Native Odoo 19:** Runs the per-registration event mail scheduler for every `event.registration`, including those created from POS orders.

**This module:** Filters out registrations with `pos_order_id` and only calls `super()` on the remainder. POS tickets are delivered by the order confirmation and batch emailer instead.

**Why it matters:** Prevents duplicate event emails for POS-sold tickets while preserving scheduler behavior for other registration sources.

### `PosOrder.send_paid_order_mail`

**Native Odoo 19 (`pos_event`):** Calls `send_paid_order_mail()` and loops through each registration to send one ticket email per registration.

**This module:** Replaces the implementation without calling `super()`. It queues the entire order into `pos.ticket.email.batch` records and triggers the cron.

**Why it matters:** Ticket PDFs are batched (25 per mail) and sent asynchronously, reducing SMTP overhead and avoiding per-registration send latency during payment.

### `PosOrder.action_pos_order_paid`

**Native Odoo 19:** Does not send an immediate order confirmation for event ticket orders out of the box.

**This module:** After calling `super()`, sends `email_template_pos_event_order_confirmation` to the first non-empty registration email for paid orders that contain event tickets.

**Why it matters:** Customers receive an immediate order confirmation with a reference number, while tickets follow later in the batch email.

### `PosStore.openOpeningControl`

**Native Odoo 19:** Displays the opening control popup asking for opening cash and notes.

**This module:** Auto-confirms with `0` cash and empty notes, skipping the dialog.

**Why it matters:** Speeds up front-desk session opening for a museum register that does not require a cash count at open.

### `OrderSummary.updateSelectedOrderline`

**Native Odoo 19:** Backspace in quantity mode decrements the line quantity to zero, requiring a second action or another confirmation step to remove the line.

**This module:** Removes the entire selected line immediately on Backspace when in quantity mode and quantity changes are allowed.

**Why it matters:** Fewer taps for cashiers removing mistaken items.

### `ProductScreen.getNumpadButtons`

**Native Odoo 19:** Exposes `-`, `discount`, `price`, and decimal-point buttons.

**This module:** Removes those buttons and reorders the remaining buttons so `Qty` sits immediately left of `Backspace`.

**Why it matters:** Simplifies the UI for a museum front desk that does not use discounts, manual prices, or negative quantities.

### `PaymentScreen.onMounted`

**Native Odoo 19:** Does not pre-select any payment method when arriving from the register.

**This module:** When `pos.payWithCash` is set, it clears any empty auto-created payment line and selects or creates the cash payment method line.

**Why it matters:** Allows a one-tap cash workflow from the mobile register.

### `Orderline` (swipe and pricelist discount)

**Native Odoo 19:** No swipe-to-delete gesture; no explicit member-pricelist strikethrough display on the orderline.

**This module:** Adds leftward swipe-to-delete on mobile, and computes a `pricelistDiscount` object comparing the pricelist unit price against the default product price.

**Why it matters:** Improves mobile cart interaction and makes active member discounts visible to the cashier without changing totals.

### `EventRegistrationPopup`

**Native Odoo 19:** Shows all ticket question forms immediately; no anonymous override workflow.

**This module:** Collapses tickets 2..N behind a `Specify Tickets` toggle, adds an `Override - Anonymous` panel with category/reason, and blocks `@memphisartmuseum.org` emails unless the override is signed.

**Why it matters:** Reduces form clutter for single ticket purchases and allows staff to override identity requirements for internal or privacy cases.

### `ReceiptScreen`

**Native Odoo 19:** Shows email/phone fields for receipt and prints badges for event registrations.

**This module:** Adds email/phone pickers populated from ticket answers, replaces the badge print flow with a `Mark as Present` / `Here` toggle, and auto-marks present when the event starts within ±12 hours.

**Why it matters:** Streamlines arrival tracking at the entrance; cashiers can pick contact information from the ticket instead of retyping it.

### `CustomerDisplay` / `CustomerDisplayPosAdapter`

**Native Odoo 19:** Shows the orderline without a member-pricelist discount line.

**This module:** Mirrors the `Orderline` patch on the customer-facing display by adding a strikethrough default price and member discount percentage.

**Why it matters:** Guests see the discount breakdown at the register.

### `EventLookupPopup` (new)

**Native Odoo 19:** No built-in order-reference lookup from the POS navbar for event registration check-in.

**This module:** Adds a `Guest Check-In` dialog that searches by `pos_reference`, lists attendees, and toggles presence.

**Why it matters:** Gives entrance staff a quick lookup without leaving the POS session.
